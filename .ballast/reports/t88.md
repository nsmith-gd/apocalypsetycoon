STATUS: DONE
- Styled rooms are now twice as big (32 pixels a side instead of 16), with much more detail. They're built on one shared room layout: a tier-trim beam, a cornice, a ceiling light, the upper wall, a dado rail, the floor, shadowed side walls, and the room's machine standing on the floor with a contact shadow. New 5-to-6-shade colour ramps went into the palette for concrete, sodium light, velvet, gold leaf, marble and walnut.
- Brutalist: board-formed concrete with clustered cast texture and broken board joints, form seams with a lit lip, tie holes with drip stains, a chipped stencilled bay number (B-3 and so on), a conduit run with brackets, a cast dado with a chamfer highlight and chips, and a polished floor with cracks. A sodium lamp throws an amber bloom and a cone of light that fades out in dither, plus an amber pool on the floor. The lamp occasionally flickers.
- Gilded: velvet damask wallpaper with gold pinstripes and worn pile, a crown moulding with dentils, a gilt-framed portrait under its picture light, a candle sconce, a chandelier whose crystals glint in turn, a gold chair rail, walnut raised-panel wainscot, and a marble checkerboard floor reflecting the chandelier.
- The machines are still re-coloured per style: cast concrete for Brutalist, brass and gold for Gilded. The needs-tier trim beam (hazard stripes, walnut, gold and emerald, shimmering violet) is identical in both styles.
- To see it: open the art gallery's "Room styles" section (every room in both styles at 4×, plus a cross-section of each at 3×), or the style demo page, where clicking a room re-skins it at the new size. Captures: 01-rooms-closeup-6x.png, 02 and 03 (cross-sections), 04 (gallery table), 05 (style demo).
- Compared against the pixel-earth target, which is the only reference listed for this task and is a map rather than a room. I matched its approach: long colour ramps, clustered shading instead of flat fills, and soft dithered transitions. The machines inside are still the old flat 16-pixel sprites with hard outlines, so they now look plainer than the rooms around them. Redrawing them is a separate job.
- Assumptions: "bigger" means each styled room is drawn at 32 pixels; the plain shelter grid elsewhere stays 16 pixels until rooms move to the new size everywhere. Brutalist and Gilded now differ in their wall details as well as their colours, because a pure recolour can't add the requested detail.
