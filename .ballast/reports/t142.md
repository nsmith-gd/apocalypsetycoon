STATUS: DONE
- The six special room rules from the GDD catalog now work in the bunker:
  - Panic Room: must touch the Master Suite (through a shared wall, floor or door), or it's worth nothing.
  - Wine Cellar: +50% prestige at 10 m or deeper.
  - Art Vault: loses 2% of its prestige every week unless a powered cable runs through it for climate control.
  - Fuel Depot: can never sit beside a bedroom. It's flagged as a fire risk and the shelter can't be handed over until it moves.
  - Infinity Pool: stays drained (no prestige) without a running Deep Water Recycler.
  - Same style: each neighbouring room in the same style (Brutalist or Gilded) adds +10% prestige, up to +30%.
- Each broken rule shows in red in the room inspector and in the shelter notes, with what to do about it; the inspector also shows any same-style bonus. Capture: panic-room-not-touching-suite-and-fuel-depot-fire-risk.png.
- Checked by one test per rule.
- Assumptions:
  - "Touching" means the two rooms share a wall, floor slab or door.
  - Climate power for the Art Vault means any powered cable network running through the room.
  - Lost art prestige doesn't come back once power is restored; it just stops falling.

## Questions
### Should a Fuel Depot beside a bedroom stop the sale, or only make it riskier?
Context: If you put a Fuel Depot next to any room with beds, the shelter shows a red fire-risk warning and the hand-over button refuses until you move it. The GDD says "never beside a bedroom" but doesn't say what happens if you do.
Options: - Block the hand-over until it moves (current) - Allow the sale but the client gains leverage and the shelter can fail a test - Stop the depot from being placed there at all
Going with: Block the hand-over, the clearest reading of "never", and the player can see why and fix it.
