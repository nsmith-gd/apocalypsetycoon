STATUS: DONE
- The Compound and the Ark are playable greyboxes in the campaign, through a new PROJECTS button in the top bar. Each site is built in the normal shelter editor: dig, wall, doors, furniture, pipes.
- Compound (unlocks at 4:20): three linked plots, sites A, B and C, each a full editor. Tunnels A–B and B–C are dug at 8 m (stays dry) or 20 m (cheaper to reach deep, but below the water table). Each week a deep tunnel may flood, and a tunnel next to a site under siege may be breached. Either one cuts the link until you pay to pump it out or reseal it. Linked sites pool their stores, and the Compound lasts as long as its weakest group, so the panel says when you're split ("2 separate groups: A+B / C").
- Ark (unlocks at 5:00): only The Table commissions it. A Table (C5) lead on the world map shows BEGIN THE ARK; more Table leads join the consortium. It's a 64-wide plot dug down to the Abyss. The checklist asks for Abyss depth (120 m), a Governance Hall, a Schoolhouse and beds for 200, and handover pays the consortium's $500M+.
- To see it: start the campaign with "?megaprojects" in the address to skip the unlock times. On the world map, open a region where you hold a Table lead and press BEGIN THE ARK, then open PROJECTS. Captures:

### Should an Ark module be its own furniture, or a room defined by ordinary furniture?
Context: Right now the Ark's Governance Hall is any room 8 m wide with a dining table and six chairs, and the Schoolhouse any room 6 m wide with four desks and a bookshelf. The checklist ticks them as soon as you furnish them. It works with the existing furniture, but the rooms don't look special.
Options: - Ordinary furniture (current): no new art needed, and it builds like any other room
- Add signature pieces (a council table, school desks and a blackboard): the rooms read as Ark modules at a glance, but need new 64-pixel art
Going with: Ordinary furniture for the greybox. Signature pieces would need new 64-pixel art, which is beyond this greybox.
