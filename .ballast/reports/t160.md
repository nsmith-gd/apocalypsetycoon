I redid the audit around your note: the game is all interface. I measured how much of each screen is actual game world. The shelter, where you spend most of the game, is 18% world, with about 3,000 characters of text and 111 buttons. The map is 43%, and a breach or the seal order is 18%. Your reference images are 85-100% world.
The biggest finding: hiding every panel doesn't help, because behind them the shelter is an empty brown rectangle at the start of a campaign. There's no surface, no estate, no crew and nobody moving. The map is a still picture, and crises live in a side list.
So v2 ranks everything by "does it put a living world on screen". There are 10 new tasks (W1-W10): a shelter scene with the estate on the surface and a starter shaft, crews you can watch digging, building you can watch, crises drawn on the map (storms, smoke, spores), city lights and traffic, breach and Midnight as moments in the world, pop-outs instead of standing panels, a HUD cut to cash and clock, and event venues as scenes. The rest is tagged to the tasks that already own it (#122, #124, #130, #32, #121, #159, R40, R41).
