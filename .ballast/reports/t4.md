## Questions
### Should a breach still double prices, now that the edge is meant to be the richest place to sell?
Context: Just below a tipping point, prices in an exposed region rise about 75% over normal, with the edge bonus. If you cross it, the breach rule from the crisis system doubles the panic premium, so the price on each sale goes up to about double. You still lose more overall: half the region's buyers flee, research slows, heat and board pressure climb, and containing it costs $10M. But the SELL button shows a bigger number after you tip the world than before.
Options: - Keep the doubling (current): breaches are lucrative but ruinous, so it's a gamble
- Drop the doubling, so the edge is the price peak and the breach is all penalty
- Keep it, but buyers keep fleeing every breach week, so a breach is a short, frantic fire sale
Going with: Keep the doubling for now, because it came from the crisis system's own brief. Overall profit still peaks at the edge once the containment bill and lost buyers are counted.

### Should the exact tipping point stay hidden until you breach, or can research reveal it?
Context: Each crisis bar shows a hatched band (for example "tips 82-94?"), and the real point is only revealed after a breach is contained. The only early warning is the EDGE note when prices jump, which tells you that you're within 10 points of the tip.
Options: - Hidden until contained (current): tense guessing; the edge bonus is your only clue
- A research project that narrows the band: rewards research with safer greed
- Heat-free "polling" scheme that reveals one crisis's exact point for a price
Going with: Hidden until contained, with the EDGE price jump as the tell. A research reveal is easy to add later.
