- Added the four pushback forces. Heat rises with every scheme: at 50 a federal investigation card appears (settle, lawyer up or stonewall), ending a quarter hot draws a regulator fine, and maxing it out brings a federal raid. Runaway tension starts feeding itself above 75, and at 100 a runaway crisis costs damages, panics clients and pulls Midnight one quarter closer.
- Every shelter buyer becomes a client in a new leverage panel. Leverage grows with your heat; past 30 clients make demands (pay for a free upgrade or take heat), past 55 they blackmail you (hush money, refund and cut ties, or call the bluff).
- The board sets a profit target each quarter that grows 25% a quarter. A miss stamps TARGET MISSED, claws back cash and adds a strike (red X marks on the HUD). Two strikes freeze the budget so builds cost 25% more; three strikes and you are ousted.
- To see it: open the pushback demo page, sell a couple of shelters, then run "Bankroll riots" and "Leak pandemic memo" until the investigation pops. Press END QUARTER a few times without selling to watch the board strikes pile up. Captures are in .ballast/media/t5 (02-heat-investigation, 03-runaway-tension, 05-client-blackmail, 06-board-target-missed, 08-ousted, plus a playthrough video).
- Assumptions: the board judges profit made this quarter, not cash on hand, so the pressure to grow never lets up (this answers the open HUD question). "Runaway tension" is one world-wide meter, separate from each region's crisis level. The schemes, sales and campaign-clock systems aren't on main yet, so the demo uses stand-in buttons; the pushback module's README lists the hooks those systems need to call.

## Questions
### Should getting ousted by the board end the run, or just cost you something big?
Context: After three missed profit targets the board votes you out. Right now a red "OUSTED BY THE BOARD" screen appears and the run is over. That's harsh in a 5-hour campaign, but it makes every target feel real.
Options: - Game over: a third miss ends the run, so the board is truly scary
- Hostile takeover: you lose half your cash and your strikes reset, and you keep playing
- Ousting forces an early Midnight: the game jumps straight to the ending with what you have
Going with: Game over for now, because it's the clearest pushback. Easy to soften once we see how long runs feel.
