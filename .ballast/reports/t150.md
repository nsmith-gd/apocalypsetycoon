## Questions
### Should a site that holds earn reputation every week, or once a quarter?
Context: Once The Pitchforks opens, every exposed site is checked every week. A well-guarded site holds every time. If each hold gave +1 reputation, three safe shelters would add about 39 reputation a quarter.
Options: - Once per site per quarter: a steady small reward for good security
- Every week: guarding becomes a reputation engine late in the game
- Only when the mob came close (within 1 point of your security): rewards living on the edge
Going with: Once per site per quarter. It keeps reputation driven mainly by the press and handovers.
