- The player can now engineer Midnight. An ENGINEER MIDNIGHT button opens a planner with two projects: Project Lights Out (unlocks around 4:50 of play) and The Final Product (around 5:30, and the board must vote 3 of 5 in favour or it's blocked, with each member's vote shown). Pick one crisis, or link two for harsher shelter requirements and a bigger fee, then set the date 4 to 12 weeks out. The planner shows exactly what shelters will need (days sealed plus critical ratings) before you commit.
- Until the date it's LAST CALL: a purple banner counts down, every sale earns triple premium and heat climbs 3 a week. A CALL IT OFF button works once and costs 20 board confidence. On the date the Seal Order runs for two weeks: a board lists every client being brought in by convoy or helicopter, with the risk from distance, sieges and clients with too much leverage, and who arrives on time, late (a quarter of the party doesn't make it in) or not at all. Then the doors seal and the ending screen shows a grade stamp (Platinum, Gold, Silver, Bronze or The Gerald; Platinum also needs an engineered Midnight with no collapses and heat under 75, and an uncontrolled Midnight drops one grade) plus the engineering fee for that grade.
- If Midnight hasn't come by the end of quarter 12, the board offers Overtime: endless extra quarters, the profit quota up 30% each one, and a new kind of crisis opening every quarter (four in all). Turning it down lets Midnight come as before. The ten apocalypses follow the design document's table (Atomic Standoff, The Bloom, Superstorm Season, The Optimizer, The Hush, The Pitchforks, The Grey Goo, The Long Shade, The Kessler Rain, The Cloud Strike), each with its required days sealed and critical needs; a linked pair needs the longer duration plus 180 days and both sets.

## Questions
### Should the engineering fee depend only on the grade, or also on how many clients made it?
Context: When Midnight lands on your date, the ending screen stamps a grade (Silver, say) and pays a fee: the project's base fee times a grade multiplier (Platinum pays 1.5x, The Gerald pays nothing). A linked pair of crises pays far more than a single one. Clients lost in the Seal Order only count through the grade.
Options: - Grade only (current): one clear stamp, one clear number
- Grade plus a per-survivor bonus: every client you got inside is visibly worth money
- Grade plus a penalty per lost client: lost pickups sting even when the grade holds
Going with: Grade only, because the grade already folds in who survived and the stamp is the moment players will remember.
