## Questions
### How strongly should a hot region cover its terrain?
Context: A region at the Edge or Tipping now gets a fairly strong amber or red wash with stripes or crosshatch, so you can spot it instantly, but you see less of the forest and desert underneath. Uneasy regions stay light.
Options: - Strong heat, terrain partly hidden (current) - Lighter wash, texture only, terrain shows through - Rim only, no fill
Going with: Strong heat, since heat is what the map is for when you're choosing where to sell or scheme.
