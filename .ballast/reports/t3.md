## Questions
### Should a sale go automatically to the richest waiting buyer, or should the player pick the client?
Context: In the region panel you see a list of waiting buyers (a celebrity, an oil heir, a politician) and one SELL button per shelter. Pressing it sells to the richest buyer, oldest first, so you never leave money on the table. It's fast, but you never choose who gets the bunker.
Options: - Richest buyer automatically (current): one click, and the map stays about where to sell
- Pick from the list: slower, but lets you choose clients for leverage or reputation reasons
- Auto by default, with a small "choose" option for players who care
Going with: Richest buyer automatically, because leverage and reputation don't vary by client yet. Picking becomes worth adding once they do.
