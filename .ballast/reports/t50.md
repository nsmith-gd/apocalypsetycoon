STATUS: DONE
- Added leads as something the player holds and spends. A lead is one person you met: the event and quarter you met them at, their name, the region they live in, their wealth class (C1 to C5), how interested they were (cold, warm or hot), the fears that brought them and any traits you noticed. Each lead has a quarter it expires in. Cold leads last only the quarter you met them, warm leads one quarter more, hot leads two more. After that they go cold for good.
- The player keeps up to 24 open leads (when full, the oldest cold one drops off). A conversation at an event becomes a lead automatically once the dialogue says the guest is interested, and the same guest can't be in the pipeline twice. A good reputation and an impressive Showroom make every new lead a bit warmer, as the design document describes.
- Selling a finished shelter on the world map can now use one of your leads as the buyer: the sale happens in that lead's region at the normal price, and the lead is used up. If a region has no other buyers, a lead from an event is the only way to sell there.
- To see it: there's no screen yet. The dialogue and lead-list screens are separate tickets, so this is the data and rules underneath them. The tests show a lead created from a conversation, held, expiring on schedule, and consumed by a world-map sale (see lead-tests.txt in the captures folder).
- Assumptions: the world map still uses its own eight regions and four price bands, so each design-document region and wealth class maps onto one of them for now (for example Coastal Megacities sells in the map's Europe region, and C3 Dynasties pay the "ultra" band). The lifetimes by interest and the 24-lead limit are my calls; the design document only gives the weekly map buyers a 4-week wait.
