STATUS: DONE
The game now draws every pixel-art picture at a whole number of real screen pixels per art pixel, on the pixel grid, with no smoothing, at 1080p, 1440p, 4K (100% and 200%), ultrawide, windowed, and on laptops set to 125% or 150%. Before, art pixels came out uneven in many places. The Doom Clock face was 1.5x everywhere. On a 125% laptop the whole map was 3.75x and the pixel font 2.5x, so letters and coastlines had uneven strokes. The title sky was stretched 10.75 x 8 on ultrawide. The shelter grid was squeezed below 1x on a 1366-wide window. Some pictures also sat half a pixel off.
To see it: on a 125% laptop the map labels and coasts are now crisp and even. Zooming the browser or dragging the window to another screen re-fits. Side-by-side crops are in `.ballast/media/t127/compare-*.png`, with full screenshots of every size.
Test note: `docs/pixel-perfect-test-note.md`. A new check measures every picture and the font at all 9 sizes: 70 problems before, 0 after.
Assumptions: if the next whole size doesn't fit, the picture steps down and leaves a dark border rather than blur, so on a 125% laptop the map is slightly smaller than before. The Doom Clock face is now 2x (64 px) instead of an uneven 1.5x. The grid and map have no free pan or zoom yet, so there is no camera to snap; the note says how to keep one pixel-perfect when it comes.
