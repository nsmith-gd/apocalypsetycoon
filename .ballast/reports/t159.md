I threw out the #126 look (brown riveted boxes, blocky 3x5 capitals, purple and ice text on brown) and rebuilt it around your reference images. The world fills the screen, and the interface is thin, lit instrument glass over it:
- Panels are near-black glass with a fine teal edge and bright corner brackets.
- The top bar is an instrument strip with big clean numbers instead of a row of boxes.
- Buttons work like neon signs: dim until you point at them, then they light up teal. Money actions glow gold, and "New campaign" is the one solid gold button.
- Lettering is two proper pixel fonts with lowercase: Jersey 10 for titles, numbers and buttons, and VT323 (a terminal font) for labels and sentences. Both are drawn at whole-pixel sizes, so they stay crisp, including on 125% and 150% laptop screens.
To see it: start a campaign and look at the top bar, the build list and the shelter panels; press 2 for the map panel and T for the threat tracks. Side-by-sides against #126: `.ballast/media/t159/compare-*.png`.
Assumptions: the two fonts are free to use (SIL Open Font License, licences included). The Midnight ending, events, dialogue and the breach card keep their current look for now, since this pass covers the menus and HUD.
