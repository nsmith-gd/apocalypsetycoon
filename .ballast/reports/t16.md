- Research now follows the GDD: five branches (Structure, Life systems, Luxury, Special Projects, Corporate) and 29 projects, tier 1 to 5. Tier 1 is bought outright with cash and lands at once (Clay digging, Blast Door Mk I, Utility Spine, Master Suite, Med Bay, a second crew). Higher tiers cost cash to start, then fill up with research points.
- Crises fund it. Every in-game week each crisis track pays a research grant by how hot it is: Simmer +2, Boil +6, Brink +15. Breached tracks pay nothing. On top of that, the panic-premium part of every shelter sale goes to the labs. The R&D screen shows last week's grant broken down by track (for example "The Hush BRINK +15"), and the top-bar chip shows RP per week. A calm world funds nothing.
- Unlocks apply everywhere. In the shelter builder, locked items are greyed and tagged R&D; digging below 10 m needs Clay digging, and the game says which project unlocks each one. Schemes on the world map unlock from Special Projects, and Spin doctors cut every scheme's heat by 25%. Extra crews come from Corporate. Client packages gain the matching rooms (hydroponics, cryo pods, wine cellar). R&D spending gets its own line in the quarter's ledger.
- The whole tree is one data file. The automated tests check that every bunker object is either in the starter kit or unlocked by a project.
- To see it: start a campaign and press R. Buy Clay digging, start Hydroponics Bay, and play on: the grants fill it as the crises heat up, and the Hydroponic rack then lights up in the build palette. Captures: 1 to 4 screenshots and research-playthrough.webm.
- Assumptions: cash costs are the GDD's costs times 0.1, matching the bunker catalog's price scale. The lab runs one project at a time; the GDD's second slot at R&D level 3 waits for departments. I used the projects that map onto rooms and schemes that exist today; the GDD's story-gated unlocks (Blueprints, the Ark, Final Product) wait for their systems. The pacing harness still passes.

## Questions
### Is a third of the starting cash too much for the first research purchases?
Context: You start with $400K. Clay digging ($150K) plus starting Hydroponics ($200K) leaves about $50K in quarter one, which is the GDD's prices at the game's current money scale. It makes the first research choice feel heavy, but it can starve early building.
Options: - Keep GDD prices: research is a real investment from day one
- Halve tier 1 and 2 cash costs: research is easier to dip into early
Going with: Keep the GDD prices for now; it is one number in the data file to change after playtests.
