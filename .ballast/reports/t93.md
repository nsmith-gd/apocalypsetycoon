- New action: CONTROLLED BURN. When a threat is at its Brink, its bar in the world map's region panel shows a red CONTROLLED BURN button. Pressing it tips that threat on purpose: it breaches at once, the game pauses on the breach card, and its containment window opens (6 weeks the first time).
- The payoff is the design document's "breach-length window of doubled premiums". While the breach is open, the premium on every sale in exposed regions doubles: both the panic premium and the edge bonus from being near the tipping point, which now stays on through a breach instead of switching off. In the test run, a shelter in the Middle East quoted $915K at the Brink (prices x1.72), $1.32M during the burn (x2.48), and $691K once contained (x1.30). The map note reads "BREACH: premiums doubled while it burns".
- The cost: heat +10 the moment you light it. When you contain it, it takes a scar (tipping point 3 lower, as with any contained breach), and the next breach on that threat gets a shorter window (4 weeks, then 3). The button's tooltip states all three. Burning also triggers everything a breach does: shelter-in-place tests, weekly heat and board-confidence hits, and collapse if you fail.
- To see it: in a campaign, push a threat to its Brink with schemes, open its region on the WORLD MAP and press CONTROLLED BURN. Sell during the window, then contain it on the breach card. Captures: at the Brink with the button, the breach card, the doubled prices on the map, prices back after containment, and the recorded numbers (the next breach window was 4 weeks). 3 new tests (370 pass).
- Assumptions: the heat cost (+10) is my stand-in, because the document says only "heat". "Doubled premiums" doubles the price premium, not the base shelter price, matching how breach weeks already worked. Only a track at its Brink can be burned, the same moment as the document's hold decision.

## Questions
### Should the controlled burn ask for confirmation before it tips the world?
Context: At the Brink, the red CONTROLLED BURN button sits under the threat's bar on the world map. One click breaches the threat at once: the game pauses, heat rises 10, and a containment clock starts. A misclick is expensive, but a confirm box slows down a deliberate, dramatic choice.
Options: - One click (current): the button's tooltip lists the costs; it feels like pulling a lever
- A confirm card ("Light it? Heat +10, 6 weeks to contain, next window 4 weeks")
- Hold the button for a second to light it
Going with: One click for now, because the move only exists at the Brink and the breach card that follows makes the stakes obvious.
