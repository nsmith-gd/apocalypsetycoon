Building in the shelter now has feedback for every step:
- **Placing:** while you place, the piece's footprint shows pulsing corner brackets, green when it fits, red and hatched when it doesn't. An order lands with brackets snapping onto the spot, a blueprint-blue flash and a puff of dust.
- **Work:** crews visibly work. Diggers swing a shovel and throw dirt in the colour of the layer they're in, builders hammer with sparks, and fitters turn a wrench.
- **Completion:** when a piece is installed a gold ring pulses out and sparkles rise. When a room comes together, its walls light up in its tier colour, the floor flashes and its name floats up ("BUNK ROOM").
- **Upgrading:** applying a style or finish (or recolouring a piece) sends a gold shimmer sweeping across the room, with sparkles behind it.
- **Demolishing:** the piece flashes red with a cross, then breaks into a dust cloud and falling debris.

To see it, open the bunker builder and place, restyle and remove things; the captures show each one in real time. It uses the existing pieces and colours; effects are short (half a second to two seconds) so building stays quick.
