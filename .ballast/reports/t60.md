- In the game you can now go to social events and talk to guests. Press EVENTS in the top bar (or E). You get the quarter's events with their cost, agenda days, crowd size, press risk and a reason if you can't go. Attend one and you see the guest list: name, wealth class, home region and fears. Pick a guest and a text conversation starts. Choices are numbered and tagged FEAR, FLATTER, PROMISE, PUSHY or RISKY, so you can see the risk before you speak. When it ends you get the result (lead or not, its warmth, plus any heat, reputation or leverage). Leaving the event gives a night's summary. The clock pauses while you're out.
- Leads you win show up straight away on the world map under YOUR LEADS HERE, ready to sell to (from #58). Everything is saved with the campaign.
- These are deliberately plain, text-only placeholder screens. They read one view-model that the final screens can reuse unchanged.
- To see it: start a campaign, press E, then 1 to attend, 1 to talk, then 1 and 1 to sell them on their fear. Back on the guest list, press 0 to leave and 1 to finish.
