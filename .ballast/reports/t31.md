## Questions
### How fast should Gil Marsh's stories reach print?
Context: In the pushback demo, Gil's story runs roughly every 6 to 9 weeks when heat is moderate, and Iris's about every 15 to 20. Each run is a big hit (heat +25, reputation -15), so with both reporters active, reputation falls quickly and a careless player can be a Pariah by mid-quarter.
Options: - Keep Gil at 2.5 times Iris's speed: constant pressure, and the press desk becomes a weekly chore
- Slow Gil to 1.5 times: stories feel like events, not weather
- Keep the speed but make Gil's stories hit softer (they're half wrong): fast and loud, but Iris is the one to fear
Going with: 2.5 times for now, so the system is easy to see in the demo. I'd tune it in the pacing harness once the campaign clock runs weeks.

### Should a mob siege happen every week, or only sometimes?
Context: Once the Pitchforks opens, each week there's a chance equal to the world tension % that a mob hits your most visible site. At tension 70 that's most weeks, so high-tension play brings constant occupations unless sites are secure or have decoys.
Options: - Chance equals tension (current): the edge is dangerous, matching "profit lives at the edge"
- Exactly one siege every week once open: predictable and easy to plan around
- One siege a month: rarer, bigger moments
Going with: Chance equal to tension, because it ties the risk directly to how hard you're pushing the world.
