## Questions
### Should the crisis colour fill the whole region, or only glow along its edges?
Context: Each region at uneasy, edge or tipping now takes a light colour tint over its whole area, with a stronger rim and a soft glow just inside its edge. Over big regions like East Asia, the tint colours the whole landmass, which is clear but makes the map quite red late in a campaign.
Options: - Whole-region tint plus rim (current): you read the crisis level anywhere on the region
- Rim and inner glow only, no tint: the terrain stays its natural colours and only the edges warn
- Tint only on tipping; uneasy and edge get rim and glow only
Going with: Whole-region tint, because the level must read at a glance, even on small regions, but I kept the tints light.
