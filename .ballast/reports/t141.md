STATUS: DONE
- Each new shelter plot now starts by choosing its product, and the product is the plot:
  - Solo Pod: 16 metres wide, up to 3 floors.
  - Family Vault: 24 wide, up to 5 floors.
  - Condo Silo: 16 wide, 8 to 12 floors, split into household units (each floor with beds is one household, 4 to 8 of them).
- The rock past the plot's edge and below its floor limit is fenced off and darkened, with the floors marked F2, F3 and so on. Digging, building or placing anything past the limit is refused with the reason ("Below the Solo Pod's 3-floor limit").
- Products unlock on the GDD schedule: Solo Pod from the start, Family Vault at 0:50, Condo Silo at 1:22.
- A Condo Silo can only be handed over once it reaches 8 floors with 4 to 8 households.
- New client contracts name the product they call for: the smallest unlocked product that fits the household. The handed-over shelter records its product, floors and units.
- How to see it: open the bunker builder on a fresh plot; the right panel says CHOOSE THE PRODUCT. Captures: 01-choose-product.png, 02-solo-pod-plot-fenced.png.
- Assumptions:
  - A floor is 4 m of depth (the shelter's floor pitch), so a 3-floor Solo Pod reaches 12 m.
  - The product is picked before breaking ground and can't be changed after.
  - Compound and Ark (multi-site and endgame) are not plot templates yet.
