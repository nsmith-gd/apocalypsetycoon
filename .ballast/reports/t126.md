The HUD, build menu, map side panel, scheme rows, threats panel, clock bar and title screen now share one theme. Labels, numbers, titles and buttons use a real pixel font made from the game's own 3x5 letters, drawn at whole-number sizes only (2x, 3x, 4x). Every panel, button and bar uses the existing bunker nine-slice frames. Spacing is on a 4px grid. Each meaning has one colour everywhere: money gold, heat purple, tension ice blue, crisis amber-to-red.
To see it: start a new campaign and look at the top bar and build menu, press 2 for the map panel, and T for the threat tracks. `?theme=off` on the URL shows the old look. Side-by-side images are `.ballast/media/t126/compare-*.png`.
No new art: the font is built in the browser from `src/art/font.js`. I added wider `$`, `M` and `W` to the on-screen font only, because in 3 pixels `$5.00M` read as "S5.00M" and "LOW" as "LOH". The frames are the existing `UI_FRAMES`.
Assumption: sentences (descriptions, dialogue, room info) stay in the mono body font, because all-caps 3x5 text is hard to read in paragraphs. Only labels and numbers switched.
Not themed yet: modals such as events, dialogue, the breach card and the seal order; they pick up the colours but keep their old boxes. This is item G7 in the #125 audit.
