- Added a pacing harness. Five scripted players (a cautious builder, an edge runner, a luxury dealer, a late arsonist who plays safe then burns the world down, and a reckless arsonist) each play 5 full campaigns through the game's real money, heat, tension, board, research and Midnight rules. Every run writes a per-quarter log of time, cash, crisis, heat, sales, fines, board result and events. The game's grid, map and scheme list aren't built yet, so stand-ins cover those.
- Result: every run that reaches quarter 12 lands at 5.8 to 6.2 hours, inside the 5 to 6.5 hour target. Both Midnight paths happen: engineered in 9 of 25 runs and failed to contain in 10. Five of the six endings showed up; only "The Ark Held" didn't. The board ousts 6 of 25 runs, mostly cautious players who never push the world hard enough to grow.
- It caught real problems in the pushback rules, which I fixed. The board demanded 25% more profit every quarter and chased any record quarter, so every player was voted out by quarter 7. It now asks for 8% growth, and after a miss it resets toward what you actually earned. Board penalties and runaway damage no longer scale without limit with your cash. A runaway crisis can now hit at most once a quarter (the reckless player used to trigger seven).
- To see it in game: in the Midnight demo press the ` key (above Tab) for the debug panel, pick a player type and press AUTO-PLAY TO MIDNIGHT. The whole campaign fast-forwards while the per-quarter log fills in. The pacing harness page shows all 25 runs on a timeline against the target band, and clicking one opens its log. ## Questions
### Should quarters run on a fixed clock, or end when the player decides?
Context: The 5 to 6.5 hour target is only met if each quarter lasts about 27 minutes of real time (pausing while you read reports and make choices). If players can end a quarter whenever they like, a full campaign with today's content takes only 2 to 2.5 hours.
Options: - Fixed 27-minute clock with pause: the target is met today, and the player feels the clock's pressure
- Player ends the quarter: more relaxed, but the runtime depends on how much the grid and map give the player to do
- Clock plus an "end early" button that costs something (lost research or sales time)
Going with: The fixed clock with pause, because it's the only option that meets the length target now, and it matches how research is already tuned.
Capture: harness-overview.png

### Should there be a way to calm the world down?
Context: Once world tension passes 75 it climbs on its own every quarter, and nothing the player can do lowers it. Every player who runs at the edge eventually suffers a runaway crisis, whatever they do. The design promises the richest zone is just below the tipping point, but right now the player can't stay there.
Options: - Add a de-escalation action (fund disaster relief, a PR spin campaign) that costs money and lowers tension
- Make the self-feeding slower so careful players can coast just below the edge
- Keep it as is: running at the edge always ends in a runaway
Going with: Recommend a de-escalation action for the schemes system. The harness already models one costing $50K for −14 tension, and with it the edge runner reaches Midnight.
