## Questions
### Should the docked ledger rail be open by default, or collapsed until the player wants it?
Context: On the main screen the right-hand rail shows the running books for this quarter and a live feed of every build, sale and scheme. It makes "two canvases, one ledger" visible all the time, but it takes about a fifth of the width from the shelter grid and the map. The top bar's ledger box already shows the net and the latest line.
Options: - Open by default (current): the money is always in view, and you feel every gold tap you buy
- Collapsed by default: more room for the canvases, with the rail one click away
- Open only on the world map: the shelter keeps full width and the map gets the books
Going with: Open by default, because the brief says the player should see the shared ledger at all times, and the rail can be collapsed with one click.
