## Questions
### On smaller screens, should the world map shrink to keep the side panels, or keep its size and push the panels away?
Context: On a 1280x720 window the world map shows at 1x, a 320-pixel-wide postcard between the Pressure panel on the left and the region panel on the right. Wider screens (1440 and up) get a crisp 2x or 3x map. There isn't room for 2x at 1280 while both side rails stay pinned.
Options: - Keep the rails and accept a small 1x map on small windows (current)
- Collapse the Pressure and Clients rails on the world map below 1440 wide, giving the map 2x
- Let the map scale to fit with uneven pixel sizes (the old behaviour; seams on the new art)
Going with: Keep the rails for now; collapsing them on small windows is the better long-term answer, but it changes the HUD layout, which is another section.
