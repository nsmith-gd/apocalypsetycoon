## Questions
### Should the player be able to change difficulty in the middle of a campaign?
Context: Difficulty is picked on the title screen before NEW CAMPAIGN and fixed for that campaign. A player who finds Hostile Takeover too harsh at quarter 6 has to start over. The design document's table doesn't say whether it can change mid-campaign.
Options: - Locked for the campaign (current): the choice means something, and the ending's grade stays comparable
- Can be lowered (never raised) from the pause menu, with the change noted on the ending screen
- Freely changeable at any time
Going with: Locked for now, because the quota and tipping points are rolled and grown from the start, and changing them midway would feel arbitrary.
