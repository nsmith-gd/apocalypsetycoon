- Added Midnight. When quarter 12 ends, every shelter you sold, plus your own headquarters bunker, is graded A to F on air, water and food against the number of people inside. Gold taps, cryo pods and wine cellars sell shelters but count for nothing here, and gold taps actually leak water.
- How Midnight arrives depends on control. With few runaway crises and low heat it is an ENGINEERED Midnight: a single, deliberate mushroom cloud on a violet sky. Lose control and it FAILED TO CONTAIN: a burning red skyline, and every shelter needs 30% more of everything to make it through.
- Six endings, chosen by the share of people who survive: Architect of the End, Managed Decline and Gilded Tombs (engineered); The Ark Held, Ashes and Receipts and Nobody Left to Bill (failed). The ending screen lights every bunker green (held), flickering amber (rationing) or dark (lost). It also shows the overall air, water and food grades and a shelter-by-shelter table that marks each one's weakest supply.

Selling only luxury bunkers ends in Gilded Tombs; selling survival bunkers ends in Architect of the End. ## Questions
### Should runaway crises make Midnight arrive early, or only make it worse?
Context: Every runaway crisis knocks 25 points off your control. Lose enough and Midnight "fails to contain": the sky burns red and every shelter needs 30% more supplies. Midnight still always arrives at the end of quarter 12, so you always get your full campaign.
Options: - Only worse (current): fixed 12 quarters, and crises make the ending harsher
- Earlier: each runaway crisis pulls Midnight one quarter closer, so you get fewer quarters to fix your shelters
- Both: an early, harsher Midnight that you can see creeping closer on the quarter track
Going with: Only worse, because the campaign length is promised at 12 quarters and an early ending would cut a 5-hour run short without warning. The game already counts how many quarters Midnight would move, so switching later is easy.
