## Questions
### How tense should a threat be on the day it opens?
Context: When a new threat opens (a toast reads "THREAT OPENS: Atomic Standoff..."), its bar on the world map starts at 20, just into the Simmer band. The Doom Clock immediately loses half a minute, because every open track's tension counts toward Midnight. Before the first threat opens at 0:25, the world is completely calm and the clock reads 11:45.
Options: - Open at 20 (current): the new threat is felt at once, and the clock ticks forward
- Open at 0: a quiet arrival, and the player's schemes are what heat it up
- Open in a band set per chapter (later threats arrive hotter), so late game ramps faster
Going with: 20, so opening a threat is a visible event on the clock without being dangerous.
