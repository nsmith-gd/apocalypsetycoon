## Questions
### Should calming the world down cost only money, or also give up profit?
Context: The new FUND RELIEF button lowers world tension by 14 for $50K, then $75K, and the press notices (heat +3). Lower tension also means lower shelter prices and fewer buyers, so relief already costs you some sales.
Options: - Money plus a little heat (current): a cheap safety valve that edge players use often
- Also cuts this quarter's sales: relief is a real sacrifice, used only in emergencies
- Only works once a quarter: one last-ditch move, no spamming
Going with: Money plus a little heat, with rising prices within a quarter. In the pacing runs that's enough to make it a deliberate choice without making the edge unplayable.
