- Added breaches. Each of the five crises (pandemic, climate, civil unrest, market crash, war) has a hidden tipping point somewhere between 82 and 94; the player only sees the possible range. When a crisis crosses it, the game pauses and a red "HAS BREACHED" card opens with a 6-week containment window (4 weeks the second time that crisis breaches, 3 after that). Every breach week costs 2 heat and 2 board confidence and doubles the panic premium.
- The player fills a 100-point containment bar from five sources: a containment op ($10M, then $20M, $30M as breaches pile up), a crash research program (research pauses meanwhile), up to three calming schemes, a government partnership (brings heat) and executive heroics (costs board confidence). Contain it and tension drops to 60, held there for 3 weeks; the exact tipping point is revealed but sits 3 lower for good. Run out of weeks and the crisis collapses at 100 and shoves its linked crises up by 15, 8 or 4 along a 10-link domino map, which can set off the next breach.
- Every breach also runs a shelter-in-place test on each shelter you've sold: shelters with enough air, water and food pass (+2 reputation); ones that don't fail (-5 reputation, and that client gains 15 leverage over you). Breaches and collapses also move the Doom Clock.
- To see it: open the breach demo, press +5 on Civil unrest until it breaches, then spend sources and advance weeks; then push Market crash over and let the window run out to watch the domino map shove its neighbours. Captures: 01 to 05 screenshots and breach-contain-collapse.webm.
- Assumptions: the full design document still isn't readable from agent sessions, so some numbers are my stand-ins, kept in one table to swap: the points each source gives (op 40, research 25, calming 10 each, government 30, heroics 15), their side costs, which crisis pairs are strong, medium or weak links, and starting reputation 50 and board confidence 60. The week-by-week test replays a scenario built from the ticket's numbers, not the design document's own example. The schemes task's tension curve isn't in the game yet; this system takes tension changes from it when it lands.

## Questions
### Should the containment card freeze the game until the player acts, or let weeks keep ticking?
Context: When a crisis breaches, the game pauses and a red card fills the screen with the containment bar, the weeks left and five ways to fill it. Weeks only pass when the player presses ADVANCE 1 WEEK. It's calm and readable, but it takes over the screen during every breach.
Options: - Full pause with the card on screen until you advance (current): no breach is ever missed
- Pause once, then shrink the card to a corner widget while the game runs on
- Never pause: a siren and a flashing crisis on the map, and the window ticks in real time
Going with: Full pause, because the ticket asks for an auto-pause and a breach is the most expensive moment in the game. A corner widget is an easy follow-up once the real game loop is in.
