STATUS: DONE
- The shelter builder's rock now has proper pixel tiles for all five layers instead of flat colour. Topsoil has crumbs and roots; Clay has smooth bands and pebbles; Bedrock has cracked, offset blocks; Deep Rock is dark with blue mineral glints; The Abyss is near-black with violet veins. Each tile varies by position, so a dig face reads as one rock wall rather than a repeated stamp.
- Construction stages are drawn in place of the old outlines:
  - Dig: a hollow opens from the top as crews work, with pick marks, a rubble pile and a bucket.
  - Shell (walls and the spine): wooden formwork and a rebar cage, then concrete poured from the bottom up.
  - Fit-out: scaffold poles and a dust sheet over the piece, which fills in as it's fitted, with sparks while the crew works.
- How to see it: open the bunker builder (Shelter tab, or the bunker demo with the starter bunker) and order some digging, walls and furniture. Captures: 01-tile-sheet.png shows every stratum and stage; 02 and 03 show a live build.
- Assumptions: L0 greybox art, procedural pixel drawing in code, no hand-made sprite sheets yet. Deep Rock and The Abyss (60 m and deeper) are deeper than the current 40 m site, so they only appear on the tile sheet for now.
