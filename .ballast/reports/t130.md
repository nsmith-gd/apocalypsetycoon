The game now has lighting and a mood that follows how close the world is to the edge, in the shelter and on the world map:
- **Calm (luxury):** warm and bright; lamps, chandeliers and the fireplace throw warm pools of light, gold taps catch a gold glow, cryo pods and aquariums glow blue; furniture sits in soft shadows.
- **Edge:** amber light, colour starts to drain, the corners close in, the luxury glows dim.
- **Runaway (dread):** red-tinted and dark, a heavy vignette, the power flickers now and then.

It moves by itself: tension sets most of it, and as the Doom Clock runs down toward Midnight the mood drags toward dread even if the world looks calm.
Settings has a new "Reduced lighting" option: no flicker or vignette, half the colour shift, softer glows.
To compare the three presets, add ?mood=calm, ?mood=edge or ?mood=runaway to the game's address (or the shelter and map demos); the captures show all three side by side for the shelter and the map, plus the reduced versions.
