## Questions

### Are the region boundaries right?
Context: each country now belongs to one sales region. Mexico and Central America are LatAm, Egypt is Mid-East, Central Asia and Siberia are E.Asia, and Indonesia and the Philippines are S.Asia. Western Russia is Europe and the rest is E.Asia. Greenland and Antarctica belong to nobody.
Options: - keep these groupings - move South-East Asia (Indonesia, Philippines, Malaysia) into Oceania - make Egypt part of Africa
Going with: these groupings, which follow common business regions (MENA, LatAm, APAC).

### Should the coast outlines be as bold as the reference's?
Context: the reference outlines every coast with a thick black line, which makes the continents pop. Ours uses a thin dark line with gaps, so the shore reads through the light-blue shallows instead.
Options: - keep the thin broken outline - go bold: a solid, thicker black coastline like the reference
Going with: the thin outline for now; bolder is a small tuning change.
