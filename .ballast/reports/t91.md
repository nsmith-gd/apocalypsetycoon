## Questions
### How much money should a new campaign start with?
Context: The GDD sets the first quarter's quota ($6M) and all prices, but never says how much cash Last Resort Holdings has on day one. With $5M the player can dig and fit out one Solo Pod and pay a quarter of crew wages before the first sale has to land.
Options: - $5M: tight, the first sale matters right away
- $10M: room for a second pod or an early scheme before the first sale
- $3M: very tight, the player must sell the first pod before building anything else
Going with: $5M, because it makes the first Solo Pod the opening goal, as the GDD's product table describes it ("the tutorial product").
