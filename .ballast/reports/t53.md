- The people you meet at events now come from 15 guest types, from The Prepping Dentist and The Lottery Winner up to The Media Mogul and The Table Envoy. Each guest has a name, a wealth class with a budget in the GDD's range ($3M for the Comfortably Nervous up to $500M+ for The Table), a home region, a dominant fear tied to one of the world's threat tracks, and two or three traits.
- Traits mix personality (vain, paranoid, skeptical, generous, gossipy, impatient, competitive, sentimental) with the GDD's client traits (engineer, germaphobe, influencer, penny-pincher, knows too much and others). Each trait says which conversation approaches the guest likes or dislikes, ready for the dialogue trees in #54. Some traits stay hidden until you talk to the guest.
- Fears follow the world: in a Grey Goo world nobody fears The Bloom; hot tracks and the event's theme make their fear more common in the room. The same campaign seed always gives the same crowd.
- See it: there is no event screen in the game yet (a separate UI ticket), so the capture is a printed guest list from two real venues in a Classic and an alternate world: sample-guest-lists.txt. Tests cover loading, the validator, seeding, budgets, fears and the event guest list.
- Assumptions: the archetypes, their weights and blurbs are mine (the GDD has wealth classes and client traits but no guest types). The GDD's "Paranoid" client trait and the ticket's paranoid personality are one trait.
