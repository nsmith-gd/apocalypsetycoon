## Questions
### Should Brutalist read as cold grey concrete or warm sandy concrete?
Context: In the gallery, Brutalist rooms are a warm beige-grey with amber lamp light, because the game's palette only has cool steel greys (already used by the plain bunker) and a warm dust grey. Warm concrete keeps Brutalist clearly different from the plain bunker look, but it reads a little sandy.
Options: - Keep warm concrete with amber light (current): clearly its own style, sodium-lit
- Add one cool concrete grey to the palette: closer to real raw concrete, but too similar to the plain steel bunker
Going with: Warm concrete, so the two styles and the plain bunker are all easy to tell apart at a glance.
