## Questions
### How much should tearing out a finished room give back?
Context: On the shelter screen, right-clicking a finished room removes it and returns half its price, with a green floating number. Cancelling a room the crew hasn't finished gives everything back. The dug-out hole stays, so building there again skips the digging cost.
Options: - Half back (current): mistakes sting but you can still rework a shelter
- Nothing back: every placement is a commitment, more tension
- Full refund: free experimentation, the grid becomes a planning tool
Going with: Half back, because it keeps "nothing is free" without punishing players who are still learning what Midnight grades.
