- The pacing harness now plays the game's real systems, wired the same way the campaign wires them. That covers the board and heat, every crisis track with breaches, containment, collapses and dominoes, the Doom Clock, Fear and buyer demand, the real scheme list, world-map prices and buyers, research funded by crisis grants, company crews, the 2:30 first-breach wildcard, the Midnight finale and telemetry. The old stand-in economy (made-up shelter prices, demand and scheme numbers) is gone from the harness. The bots now only make player decisions: which schemes to run, what to build and research, and how to answer the board's cards.

  - PASS: campaign length 5.88 h median (target 5-6.5)
  - PASS: quotas hit in 72% of quarters (about 75%)
  - MISS: natural first breach by 2:30 in only 20% of runs (target 60%)
  - PASS: first breaches land in the 2:10-2:30 window in 80% of runs that breach
  - PASS: first breaches contained 80% (target 70%)
  - PASS: engineered vs uncontrolled Midnight 55/45 (target 60/40)
  - MISS: median grade Bronze (target Silver)
  - MISS: research unlocks within 30 minutes of the GDD schedule for 7 of 10 nodes; Hydroponics, Deep Water Recycler and Think Tank arrive 33-46 minutes early

Another: cash snowballs (an edge runner ends near $23M) because a finished shelter sells for far more than it costs to build. Also, runs within one player type barely vary by seed, because the real systems use far less randomness than the old stand-ins.
