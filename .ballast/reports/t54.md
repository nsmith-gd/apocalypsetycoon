STATUS: DONE
- The game now has a general engine for conversations with guests. A conversation is a set of lines and choices. A choice can be hidden until a condition holds: a trait the guest has, your company's reputation, your current heat, how hot a crisis is (by default the one the guest fears), how much the guest trusts you, or things said earlier. Lines can also change with the world ("Be quick. Have you seen the news?" when their fear is boiling).
- Everything a choice does (trust, heat, reputation, flags, revealed traits, promises, slips) goes through one channel to the rest of the game. Every conversation ends in one of the existing end states (sold the fear, sold the dream, hard close, insider tip, polite exit, rebuffed, backfire), which feed the lead rules already in the game.
- Writers can author a conversation as a short text script instead of code; a checker reports broken links, dead ends, loops with no way out and typos, with line numbers.
- See it: no new screen (the event screens other agents built still use their own small runner, which this engine can replace next). The capture headless-runs.txt shows one example guest played three ways: a calm world, a trusted company that unlocks extra lines, and a low-heat world with a boiling crisis that opens a riskier route. Tests run all 13 authored guest conversations through the engine.
- Assumptions: trust starts at 0 and choices can be marked "ask once". Conditions read reputation, heat and crisis levels live, so a conversation reacts if they change mid-talk.
