- Added a research tree with three branches: 6 amenities (hydroponic garden, deep-well recycler, cryo pods, military air scrubber, wine cellar, AI butler), 4 new schemes (think tank, deepfake, engineered shortage, grid blackout) and 3 extra build crews.
- Crises fund it. Research income climbs steeply with crisis (about 0.75/s at a calm 22, 3.1/s at 71, 4.5/s at the peak just below the tipping point). Past the tipping point it drops, because the labs get caught in the chaos. A small curve in the R&D screen shows where you sit.
- A RESEARCH chip on the top bar shows income and the current project, and blinks orange when the labs are idle. Press R for the full tree. Finishing a project pops a "Research complete" note, and the unlock appears in the build palette (amenities), the scheme bar (schemes) or the crew count.
- To see it: open the prototype page (demo/hud-demo.html), press R, pick Hydroponic garden, run a few schemes to raise tension and watch it finish. The garden then lights up in the build palette. Captures: 1-palette-locked.png to 6-research-phone.png, research-unlock-playthrough.webm.
- Assumptions: world crisis is read from the tension meter until the world map system provides a real crisis level. Research is one project at a time, and points earned while idle are banked. Costs are tuned for roughly 27 real minutes per quarter. The prototype runs at x10 speed, which you can toggle.

## Questions
### Should research slow down once the world tips past the edge?
Context: The R&D screen shows research income rising as the world gets worse. Right now it peaks just below the tipping point, then falls to about 40% at full collapse. That echoes "the richest zone sits just below each tipping point", but it also punishes players who overshoot.
Options: - Falls past the tipping point: research rewards running it close, not over
- Keeps rising: more chaos is always better for research, and heat and tension do the punishing
Going with: Falls past the tipping point, because it makes the edge itself the sweet spot.
