- When a threat breaches, clients in the regions most exposed to it now move into the shelters you sold them, and each shelter is tested for real. The test checks the shelter's own Days Sealed against the contract and its ratings against that threat's critical ratings: Atomic Standoff needs 730 days, blast 4 and seal 3; The Bloom needs seal 4; The Pitchforks needs security 4, and so on. Shelters in regions only lightly exposed are left alone. Before, every shelter anywhere was tested on air, water and food alone.
- Pass: reputation +2. Fail: reputation -5, and that client gains 15 leverage over you. A bad failure (survival odds under 50%) also loses occupants, in proportion to how badly it fell short. A build still under construction in that region loses a week of work instead: the crew is sent home.
- The breach card now lists the results under SHELTER-IN-PLACE, with the reason for each failure (for example "90 of 730 days sealed, blast 1/4, seal 1/3").
- To see it: start a NEW CAMPAIGN with "?preset=breach" added to the address. Three shelters are sold, two in the Middle East and one in Europe, and a moment later Atomic Standoff breaches. On the card, The Granite Keep passes (+2), Marble Folly fails (-5 reputation, +15 leverage, 4 of 8 occupants lost), and the European shelter isn't tested. Captures: the card screenshot and the recorded results. 4 new tests (280 pass).
- Assumptions: no contract model yet holds a required Days Sealed, so a shelter is held to the breaching apocalypse's own duration unless it carries a contract number (a contract asking for less passes on less). Occupants lost = the shelter's size times how far its survival odds fall below 50%, at least one. The HQ dig in North America is the only "under construction" site in the campaign today. The breach system still keeps its own reputation number, separate from the press desk's; merging them is outside this ticket.
