Client Gaze and photo mode now have art at the 64-pixel minimum, and both can be tried in the bunker builder.
Client Gaze (VIEW: CLIENT GAZE in the builder's view menu) shows what a client will look at on a walkthrough:
- Status and Delusion rooms get gold spotlight brackets and a wide, glancing eye.
- Comfort rooms get dashed amber brackets and a heavy-lidded eye (half the time).
- Survive rooms are dimmed with a shut, sleeping eye.
- There is also an inspector eye (magnifying glass) for clients who check Survive rooms, and footprints for the walkthrough route.

Photo mode (the PHOTO button or O) hides the interface, puts the shelter on a black screen with white viewfinder corners, a PHOTO MODE plate, a shutter-iris icon and faint thirds guides. ENTER fires a warm flash and pops up a polaroid share card of your rooms signed "Last Resort Holdings · Your shelter. Your terms."; ENTER again goes back to framing, ESC leaves.
Every asset is at least 64 by 64 and passes the minimum-size check. In the builder, which still uses the 16-pixel grid, the gaze marks draw a reduced copy, as the furniture does. The photo-mode pieces draw at full size on the screen.
Compared against the "pixel art fidelity and LOQ" references (ref 1 and 2) and the cards-and-events reference (ref 3) for the share card: same outline and top-left light as the 64 px rooms; the card is cleaner and less textured than ref 3's paper.
Assumption: no client is chosen in the builder yet, so the gaze view shows the default walkthrough (no Inspector). The real walkthroughs (who tours, at 50% and 100% fit-out) are the gameplay ticket for Client Gaze.
