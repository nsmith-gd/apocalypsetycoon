Each card shows its week, its type (Headline, Opportunity, Threat, Client, Wildcard) and its track and band, with a line of flavour. There are two or three buttons, each listing exactly what it does, e.g. "-$80K · The Hush -6 · reputation +3". Press 1, 2 or 3, or click.
