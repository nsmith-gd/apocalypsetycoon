STATUS: DONE
- Rooms now work the way the director described. A room is any space enclosed by walls, and what's inside decides which room it is. Every one of the GDD's 59 enclosed rooms has its definition in the catalog: e.g. a Master Suite needs a four-poster bed, an Art Vault two oil paintings, a Library a bookshelf plus a pool table or arcade. The shelter grid reads these definitions for every room; its old hand-written room list is gone.
- A room has one identity at a time. When its contents fit several rooms, the most demanding definition wins, then the one with more of its defining objects inside, then the higher needs tier, then the more valuable objects. So a stove and a fridge make a Galley Kitchen, not a Pantry; a four-poster and a double bed make a Master Suite; two generators beside a battery make a Diesel Generator room. Contents that fit no room make a Storeroom.
- Clicking a room with INSPECT now also says what's missing for the nearest room it isn't yet, e.g. "Add 1x Oil painting to make it an Art Vault."
- Doors, blast doors, linings, flood seals, the Utility Spine and surface builds (solar mast, helipad, security post...) are still in the 73-entry catalog, but are now marked as fixtures, a structure or surface builds rather than rooms.
- See it: open the bunker demo with the furnished suite (bunker-demo.html?suite). Room names come from what's inside them; click INSPECT then a room. Captures 01 to 04.
- Assumptions: size isn't part of a room's definition. A room narrower than the GDD size is still that room, just flagged "cramped" as before. 29 of the 59 rooms can be built today. The other 30 (Throne Room, Spa, Panic Room...) name furniture the grid doesn't have yet, so they can't appear until that furniture is added. Washroom (toilet, shower, sink or bath) is kept as an extra room the GDD doesn't list.

## Questions
### Should stray furniture stop a room from counting as its type?
Context: Right now a room is whatever its defining objects say, and anything else inside just sits there. A Bunk Room with a pool table in it is still a Bunk Room.
Options: - Keep it: extra furniture never changes or breaks a room's identity
- Mixed rooms lose their identity (a "Cluttered room") unless every object belongs to the room type
- Extra furniture is allowed but lowers the room's impressiveness
Going with: Keep it. Players can decorate freely, and the inspector's hint already shows what a room is closest to.
