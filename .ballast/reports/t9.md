## Questions
### How loud should crisis look on the world map?
Context: On the world map, each region's crisis level paints a dithered overlay over the land: gold dots, then orange, then a shimmering red pattern. With most regions near the edge the map turns mostly red and orange, which feels dramatic but hides the terrain underneath.
Options: - Keep the bold dither: crisis is unmistakable at a glance
- Lighter dither plus a coloured border per region: terrain stays visible and crisis reads from the outline
Going with: The bold dither for now, because "the world is on fire and you did it" is the core fantasy.
