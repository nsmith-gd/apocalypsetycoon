## Questions
### Should the board target measure cash on hand or profit made this quarter?
Context: The top bar shows a board target, and the quarter report stamps it MET or MISSED. Right now it compares the cash you hold at quarter end against the target. That means a big early windfall can carry you through several quarters.
Options: - Cash on hand: simple, but hoarding cash can satisfy the board
- Quarterly profit: the board wants growth every quarter, so the pressure never lets up
Going with: Cash on hand for now, because it is the simplest thing to read. It is easy to switch once the board system lands.
