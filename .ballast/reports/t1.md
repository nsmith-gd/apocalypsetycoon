## Questions
### Should the player be able to end a quarter early?
Context: Right now an END QUARTER button sits in the clock bar, so a player who has nothing left to do can skip the rest of the 27 minutes. That makes the campaign shorter than the 5 to 6.5 hour target for anyone who uses it a lot.
Options: - Keep the free button: players never wait around, but runs can be much shorter
- Hide it outside of testing: the clock always runs its full length
- Keep it but charge for it (lost research time or a board penalty)
Going with: Keep the free button for now, since there isn't yet enough to do in a quarter to fill 27 minutes; revisit once the grid and map land.
