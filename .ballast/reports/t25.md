STATUS: DONE
- Added the Doom Clock. It sits on the top bar as a little pixel clock face: the hand starts at 11:45 and creeps toward twelve as world tension rises. A red wedge marks time lost for good, because the clock's floor only ever moves toward Midnight. It drops a quarter-minute the first time each crisis reaches its brink, half a minute each chapter, a minute for every breach you contain and four minutes for a collapse. The box shakes whenever the floor drops.
- Every minute lost also makes the world more fragile: every crisis's tipping point falls by one (never below 72), so the same scheme tips the world sooner late in the game. A second collapse starts the FINAL COUNTDOWN: the clock turns red and blinks "Midnight in 4 weeks". If the hand reaches twelve, or the countdown runs out, Midnight comes uncontrolled: the twelve tolls play and the ending screen says the clock struck before you were ready.
- To see it tick: open the Doom Clock demo and press AUTO-PLAY. Five crises drift hotter week by week, some breaches get contained and some collapse; watch the hand, the red wedge and the falling tipping points until the Final Countdown and Midnight. The clock is also on the top bar of the full campaign prototype. Captures: 01 to 07 screenshots and doom-clock-ticking.webm.
- Assumptions: the crisis tracks, Brink, breach and collapse rules belong to the schemes task, which isn't on main yet. Until then, the demo uses five stand-in crises (Brink = within 10 of the tipping point; breach = past it; collapse = an uncontained breach that reaches 100), and the campaign prototype treats world tension as one crisis and each runaway as a contained breach. Chapters start at quarters 4, 7 and 10; a quarter is 13 weeks.

## Questions
### Should the player see how much each event will cost on the Doom Clock before it happens?
Context: Right now the clock face shows the time, minutes left and the floor. The player only learns that a collapse costs four minutes when the red wedge jumps. The GDD numbers are fixed, but nothing on screen warns you a collapse will eat a quarter of your remaining time.
Options: - Keep it hidden: the wedge jumping is a shock, and players learn by losing time
- Tooltip on the clock listing what moves the floor (brink, chapter, contained breach, collapse)
- Warn on each crisis at its brink: "collapse here costs 4 minutes"
Going with: A plain tooltip on the clock for now ("the red wedge is time you can never win back"). Per-crisis warnings fit better once the real crisis tracks exist.
