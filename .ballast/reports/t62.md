## Questions
### Should a style mismatch on a room the client never looks at still count?
Context: In the style demo, a Gilded client ignores the style of the tanks, vents and pantry, because the design document says only Paranoid or Engineer clients inspect Survive rooms. So you can leave your plumbing in bare concrete and lose nothing with most clients.
Options: - Only rooms the client looks at count (current): saves money on plumbing, rewards knowing your client
- Every room counts: a full re-style matters, but survival rooms become a style chore
Going with: Only rooms they look at, because it matches the design document's Client Gaze rule and keeps the "they don't look at the plumbing" joke.
