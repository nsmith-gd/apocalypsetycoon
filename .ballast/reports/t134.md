## Questions
### Should the borders between neighbouring regions follow natural features instead of straight lines?
Context: On the world map, where two regions share a continent (Europe and East Asia, the Middle East and Africa), the line between them is perfectly straight, and selecting the Middle East shows a gold box with square corners. The coasts around them are now organic, so these straight lines stand out.
Options: - Keep straight lines (current): reads like a game board, and it's clear where one region ends
- Redraw them along rivers and mountain ranges in the painting: looks like a real atlas; needs a new region layout drawn on the map
- Soften the corners only: rounded steps instead of square ones, same regions
Going with: Straight lines for now, since changing the region shapes changes where players click; I'd recommend the redraw as a follow-up art ticket.
