## Questions
### Should the guest list show each guest's fear up front, or make the player find it in conversation?
Context: When you walk into an event, the guest list shows each person's type and what they're afraid of (for example "fears The Hush"). That makes the right opening line easy to pick: playing to their real fear raises trust and makes a hotter lead. The conversations also have lines that guess the wrong fear and cost trust.
Options: - Show fears on the list (current): reading the room is about picking the right pitch
- Hide fears until you ask or a line reveals them: more guessing, and wrong guesses cost you
- Show a fear only for guests you've researched (an intel scheme), tying events to schemes
Going with: Show them for now, because the event system already reveals them. Hiding them is a one-line change if conversations should be more of a gamble.
