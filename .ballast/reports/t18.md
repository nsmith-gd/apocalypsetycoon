- Every quarter now ends with a board meeting. Five board members each have their own confidence. They judge your revenue against a quota that grows 30% a quarter, as in the GDD: $150K in Q1, about $330K by Q4 and $2.7M by Q12 in today's money.
- The quarter report shows three things: quota met or missed, how the two directives went (e.g. "Push any track to the Brink", "Keep heat below 40"), and the board's mood. It then names next quarter's quota and the two new directives. A toast at the start of each quarter repeats them.
- How confidence moves: hitting the quota +5, beating it by 25% +10, missing it −15. A fulfilled directive is +8 with the member who set it, a failed one −5. Breaches anger the Chair and Priya.
- What the mood does: Bullish brings an investment round of cash. Scrutiny makes schemes 20% hotter. Probation sends a Minder who vetoes your first scheme each quarter. Zero confidence means you're ousted and the campaign ends. Mr. Gray takes his seat in Q11.
- The old "three strikes" target is gone from the campaign, and the left Pressure panel now shows the revenue quota and the board's mood. To see it, start a campaign and press N to end quarters. Captures 01 to 04 show quarters 1 to 4 played with no sales.
- Not yet: there's no button to refuse a directive (the rule exists, -3 with that member), and votes, Favours and tier-ups aren't on screen yet. Mr. Gray's "Begin the Ark" can't be asked for until the Ark exists.
- Assumptions: everyone starts at 60 confidence (not in the GDD); the quota is the GDD curve scaled to today's $K prices (one number to flip when prices move to $M); breach-week dents from the crisis system carry into the board.
