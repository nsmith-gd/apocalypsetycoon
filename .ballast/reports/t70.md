- When the Doom Clock reaches zero, or the Final Countdown runs out after a second collapse, the game now takes the uncontrolled road to Midnight instead of jumping straight to a score. The clock strikes and the Seal Order board scrambles every client you sold to into their shelter, with no Last Call and no date of your choosing. Distance from your HQ, sieges in their region and clients holding too much leverage make them late or lost. Then every shelter is graded against the apocalypse that actually arrived.
- The apocalypse is the one you caused. After a countdown it's the two tracks that collapsed, combined: the longer sealed duration plus 180 days, and both sets of needs. If the clock struck on its own, it's the hottest open track. The ending screen is headed OFF THE RAILS with that apocalypse's name, and the grade drops one step ("UNCONTROLLED, -1"), as the design document says. Shelters built on the tile grid are now judged on their own Days Sealed and ratings after being sold; before, those numbers were lost at the sale.
- To check it from a debug preset: start a NEW CAMPAIGN from the main menu with "?preset=countdown&qs=26" added to the address. Every threat is open, three shelters are sold, and two crises have collapsed, so the Final Countdown shows 4 weeks. Four in-game weeks later (about 2 seconds each with that setting; answer the weekly news cards as they come) Midnight strikes. With "?preset=clockzero" the floor falls to zero 3 seconds in. In my runs the countdown preset ended Off the Rails against Atomic Standoff + The Bloom: the Deep Vault all survived, the Gold Tap Grotto was lost, and the result was graded The Gerald (40%). - Assumptions: Seal Order distance is measured from the HQ in North America (0 to 3 steps), and a region counts as besieged when a track it's highly exposed to is at 90 or more; both are my stand-ins. The end of quarter 12 without Midnight still grades straight away; Overtime and the engineered road aren't wired into the campaign yet. The older Midnight rule that uncontrolled shelters need 30% more of everything still applies on top of the one-grade drop. The Dark, epilogue cards and the final ledger from the design document's Midnight sequence are later Midnight work.

## Questions
### Should an uncontrolled Midnight cost both a grade and 30% more supplies, or only the grade?
Context: On the Off the Rails ending, the screen says "Every shelter needed 30% more to survive" and the grade stamp also reads "UNCONTROLLED, -1". The design document mentions only the one-grade drop. The 30% rule came from the first Midnight build, before the design document was readable. Together they make losing control very punishing: in the preset run only the best-built shelter survived.
Options: - Keep both (current): losing control is a disaster, so engineering Midnight is clearly the goal
- Grade drop only, as the document says: an unplanned Midnight still hurts, but well-built shelters fare as they deserve
- Supplies penalty only, with no grade drop
Going with: Keep both for now, because changing Midnight's survival rule belongs to the Midnight feature. Dropping the 30% is a one-number change once you decide.
