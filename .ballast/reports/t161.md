- **Government contracts:** when a threat is at its Brink, each region exposed to it is offered a government contract: "6 volume shelters for the state", low prestige. Signing pays $2.1M and cools heat by 1. An unsigned offer lapses after 4 weeks.
- **Kickbacks:** Arms Broker and Gentrify are now fundable schemes, and their kickbacks arrive the following week ($2M back, and $3M in land).
- **Revenue streams:** every bit of income is tagged as contracts, retainers, change orders, government, kickbacks or investment rounds. Press $ in game (or add ?debug=revenue to the URL) for a readout of each stream's share against the chapter 3 targets of 55/15/10/10/5/5.
To see it: push a threat to its Brink, open an exposed region on the map, and press SIGN. Heat drops by 1 and the government share appears in the readout. Captures: 01-government-contract-offered.png, 02-signed-heat-down-mix-updated.png, 03-arms-broker-kickback.png.
Assumptions:
- The contract size and price are placeholders; the design doc gives none.
- The state builds the volume shelters itself, so a contract pays at signing and takes none of your crews or grid space.
- Retainers and change orders are tagged but nothing in the game pays them yet, so they read 0% until that income exists.
- Arms Broker and Gentrify are available from the start rather than unlocking at 2:10 and 3:30 of play.
