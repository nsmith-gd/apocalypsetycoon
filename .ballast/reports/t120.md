Every room in the game can now be shown in all six design styles at the new 64-pixel minimum: Brutalist, Gilded, Biophilic, Tech-Minimal, Retro-Atomic and Maximalist Kitsch.
Each style is a real re-skin, not a tint:
- Brutalist: board-formed concrete and sodium lamps.
- Gilded: marble in gold frames over velvet, with chandeliers.
- Biophilic: a living moss-and-fern wall over timber slats.
- Tech-Minimal: white panels, a hidden screen and LED coves.
- Retro-Atomic: mint tile, a chrome band, starbursts and sputnik lamps.
- Kitsch: hot-pink lacquer, leopard print, a mirrored ceiling and neon.

The furniture and machines are re-skinned in each style's materials (a gold sofa, a teal fridge, a velvet blast door), while the needs-tier stripe along the top never changes. Outdoor rooms show the style in their terrace paving and railing.
In game, the room card in the bunker builder now shows the room in the style the player gave it. The style picker still offers only Brutalist and Gilded until the other four get their wall, floor and furniture kits.
The old 32-pixel style template is retired; the design-layer demo and the art gallery now show 128-pixel rooms in all six styles.
Compared against the "pixel art fidelity and LOQ" references (ref 1 and 2): same 64 px per metre, lit with the same lamp cones and shadowed corners as the #117 room tiles. The new walls are less worn and grimy than the references, and Kitsch and Retro read flatter than the others.
