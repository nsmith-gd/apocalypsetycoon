Pitch
You don't survive the apocalypse. You sell it.

Overview
Apocalypse Tycoon is a pixel-art management sim about building luxury doomsday shelters for
the ultra-wealthy while quietly stoking the crises that sell them. One continuous campaign runs
5 to 6.5 hours across 12 in-game quarters and ends at Midnight: the apocalypse you either
engineer on your terms or fail to contain.

Player fantasy
You found Last Resort Holdings, a shelter builder with two shovel crews and a board that expects
growth every quarter. Calm worlds don't buy bunkers, so you learn to make the world a little
less calm. The whole game asks one question: how close to the edge do you dare run it?

Design pillars
1. Profit lives at the edge. Crises fund your research and inflate your prices, and the richest
   zone sits just below each tipping point.
2. Two canvases, one ledger. The shelter grid is what you build and the world map is what you
   sell. Every scheme changes demand, and every sale needs more crisis.
3. Build what they want, grade what they need. Clients pay for gold taps and cryo pods. Midnight
   grades air, water and food.
4. Nothing is free. Heat, runaway tension, client leverage and the board push back on every
   move, so power always feels precarious.

Systems named in the brief (from the task list)
- Project skeleton, game loop and 12-quarter campaign clock
- Shelter grid: place rooms and amenities
- World map with regional demand, crisis levels and shelter sales
- Crisis-stoking schemes with tipping points and the profit-at-the-edge curve
- Pushback systems: heat, runaway tension, client leverage and board expectations
- Midnight ending graded on air, water and food
- HUD, quarter report and ledger screen (cash, quarter, heat, tension, board target; ledger
  lines for builds, sales, schemes and fines; grid / world map toggle)
