ENDINGS: DEBUG PANEL (#32)

Each button makes that ending's trigger true in the ending watcher, checks which ending it picks, then plays the end of the campaign: epilogue cards, The Ledger, and the ending card. The sample campaign has 10 clients (6 hero clients and Gerald among them) in shelters of mixed quality, two breaches, one collapse and heat 62. In a running campaign, open the browser console and type __game.endings.end('indicted').
