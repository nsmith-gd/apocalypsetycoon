LAST RESORT 28: ART STYLE GUIDE

Every pixel in Apocalypse Tycoon comes from this page's palette and sprite sets. All art is drawn at 1x and shown only at whole-number scales (1x, 2x, 3x...), never stretched or smoothed.

PALETTE

SHELTER TILES & AMENITIES (16PX)

Light from the top-left. Objects carry a 1px near-black outline; terrain doesn't. Luxury uses gold and chrome. Survival kit (air, water, food) is utilitarian steel with a single accent colour: mint, blue, green.

SHELTER CROSS-SECTION (3X)

WORLD MAP (8PX TERRAIN)

A retro war-room map. Terrain is low-contrast so crisis marks read on top. Each region's crisis level shows as a dither overlay: none when calm, sparse gold when uneasy, orange at the edge, shimmering red at the tipping point.

CRISIS LEVELS ON THE MAP (2X)

UI FRAMES & ICONS

Nine-slice frames tile their edges, never stretch. Dark riveted panels for the bunker, paper for reports.
